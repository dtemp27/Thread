Quick answers

Common questions

Is there a subscription or monthly fee?

No. You pay once for your piece and that's it. No monthly fees, no hidden charges. Your QR code is active for life.

Do I need to post on social media?

Nope. The QR is on your shirt — it works in the real world. You just wear it. Anyone near you can scan it.

When do I get paid?

Commissions accumulate in your dashboard. Payouts are processed once you hit the minimum threshold. You can track every scan and sale in real time.

Can I have more than one piece?

Yes — and it helps. More pieces means more QR codes out in the world simultaneously. Your tee earns at the gym while your hoodie earns on the train.

What if no one scans my QR?

You still have a quality piece of clothing. But between school, work, transit, and social settings, most wearers start seeing scans within the first week.
